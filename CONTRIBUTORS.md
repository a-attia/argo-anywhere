## On AI-collaborator authorship

This project's commits authored or co-authored by an AI are explicitly
labeled. The intent is twofold:

1. **Transparency for code review.** Reviewers should know when reading
   a diff whether the change was hand-written by a human or
   substantially generated by an AI under human direction. The two have
   different failure modes and warrant different review attention.

2. **Provenance for future audits.** As LLM-assisted code authoring
   becomes more common in scientific software, a clear paper trail of
   "what was AI-assisted, by which model, in what session" supports
   reproducibility and accountability work.

For background on Anthropic's `claude-code` CLI and its co-authorship
convention, see the [Claude Code documentation](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code).
